# Objection Handling, Stage Overage & Acceleration Playbook

## Stage Overage Recovery Tactics
When a deal exceeds 1.6× historical stage median duration (stage_overage signal):
1. **Diagnosis**: Determine whether stagnation is due to champion disempowerment, procurement/legal bottleneck, or unstated competitor evaluation.
2. **Re-scoping**: If Proposal stage duration exceeds 22 days, schedule an executive alignment call to validate that problem urgency and business case have not eroded.
3. **Stage Recalibration**: If no tangible progress occurs within 7 business days of overage detection, downgrade deal stage or apply a 30% pipeline haircut.

## Multi-Threading & Stakeholder Mapping
Deals single-threaded on a technical champion face severe slippage risks:
- Introduce user buyer and economic buyer before advancing past Discovery.
- Connect sales engineering leads directly with technical evaluators to isolate integration hurdles.
- When legal or security reviews stall, request a 30-minute joint redline working session rather than asynchronous email exchanges.

## Pricing Objections and Give-Get Negotiation
- Never provide unconditioned concessions or discounts to accelerate close date without an executive give-get.
- Valid give-gets: multi-year prepayment, case study / reference commitment within 90 days of go-live, or reduced custom SLA scope.
- Involve RevOps and executive sponsors early on contracts over $150,000 ACV.
